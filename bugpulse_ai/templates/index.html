Enterprise Architecture & Evaluation Rubrics

4-Pillar Second-AI Quality Judge Formulation

Every generated summary undergoes automated verification by an independent judge evaluator before being admitted into production:

Q_total = 0.35 * S_semantic + 0.25 * E_entity + 0.20 * C_structure + 0.20 * F_faithfulness
• S_semantic: Coverage of core failure mechanism and system impact
• E_entity: Recall of critical error codes (e.g. 504, SocketTimeoutException)
• C_structure: Adherence to the mandatory 6-block structured schema
• F_faithfulness: Epistemic grounding in source line quotes without hallucination
